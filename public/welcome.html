Good to know

Questions, answered honestly

Where do the numbers come from, and how accurate are they?

Views, likes and comments are Instagram's own public counts, collected through Apify, a third-party data service, at the moment you fetch. Remixfox does not estimate or change them. They keep growing after a fetch, so fetch again for fresh numbers. If a creator hides their likes, Instagram does not share them and they show as 0.

How often is competitor data updated?

Whenever you ask: press Fetch Reels or Refresh. Each competitor can be fetched once every 24 hours, which brings its 20 latest reels (pinned reels are skipped; the 5 most viewed on the free trial) with fresh numbers. Nothing runs in the background, so your plan is only used when you want it to be.

Which Instagram accounts can I analyse?

Any public Instagram account that posts reels: paste its link or @username. Private accounts cannot be fetched. Remixfox never asks for your Instagram password and never connects to or posts from your account.

What if Instagram data cannot be fetched?

What we can fetch depends on what Instagram makes public and on Apify, so now and then a fetch fails or brings fewer than 20 reels: for example, when an account is private, renamed or has no reels, or Instagram limits access for a while. The activity panel shows what happened. A failed fetch counts no reels and does not start that competitor's 24-hour wait, so you can try again; every plan has two spare fetches a day for this.

Which AI does Remixfox use?

Transcripts come from speech-to-text through Apify. The hook, script and call to action, the translations into your language and the remixes in your voice are written by OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna, through OpenAI's API. AI can get things wrong, so read each script before you film it.

Can I just copy my competitors' reels?

No, and you do not need to. Reels belong to their creators: Remixfox shows you what works (the hook, the structure, the pacing) and helps you write your own version for your business. Do not republish other people's videos or their words.

What happens when the free trial ends?

After 15 days, or once its 4 fetches are used, fetching new reels needs a plan. Everything you fetched, your scripts and your content plan stay in your account.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, at any time: Plans page → Manage billing and invoices. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for and are not charged again. A new paid plan also has a 14-day right to cancel; the details are in our Terms of use.
