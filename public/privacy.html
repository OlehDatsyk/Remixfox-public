Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Who we are

Remixfox (remixfox.co.uk) is run by Oleh Datsyk, a sole trader, in the United Kingdom. We are the controller of your personal data. For anything about your data, write to oleh@remixfox.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your account: your e-mail address if you sign in by e-mail or with a password; or, if you sign in with Telegram, your Telegram user ID, name, @username and profile photo.
  • Signing in: one-time codes (stored only in scrambled form and valid for 10 minutes), your password if you set one (stored only as a one-way scrambled hash, never as the password itself), and a session cookie that keeps you signed in.
  • What you add: the Instagram accounts you track, and what we fetch about their public reels (captions, numbers such as views and likes, cover images and transcripts), the AI's scripts and translations, plus your own scripts and content plan, and the description of you that you write under Settings → Your voice.
  • Usage: how many reels you fetch and videos are analysed, with their estimated cost, so we can apply your plan's limits.
  • Payments: Stripe handles payments. We keep your Stripe customer and subscription references, your plan and its status, never your card details.
  • Technical data: IP addresses in server logs, and to limit how often sign-in codes and passwords can be tried.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you signed up for: your account, reels, scripts and content plan (performance of a contract).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse (our legitimate interests).
  • To take payments and keep the records the law requires (legal obligation).

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Who helps us

These providers process data for us, only to run Remixfox:

  • Render: hosting and database (servers in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes by e-mail.
  • Telegram: if you choose to sign in with Telegram.
  • Stripe: payments and subscriptions.
  • Apify: fetches public data about the Instagram reels you ask for, and their transcripts.
  • OpenAI: turns reel transcripts into a hook, script and call to action, translates them and the captions into the language you choose, and writes remixes in your voice from the description you give.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

Inside Remixfox, only the owner and up to ten support admins the owner appoints can see account details (name, e-mail, plan) and fetch history, and only to run the service and help you.

Cookies

We use only cookies that are needed for Remixfox to work: remixfox_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and remixfox_tg lasts 10 minutes while you sign in with Telegram. Your browser also remembers small preferences, such as light or dark theme, which reels you picked and whether you have seen the guide. There are no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, so we do not ask for consent to any.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, competitors, reels, scripts, content plan and usage records: while your account is open. When you delete your account or ask us to, we delete them within 30 days.
  • Billing records (invoices, payments and the subscription details needed for them): six years after the end of the financial year they belong to, as UK tax law requires. Stripe keeps its own records under its policy.
  • Server logs (requests, IP addresses and errors), kept by our hosting provider: up to 30 days.
  • Signing in: codes and Telegram sign-in links expire after 10 minutes, and sessions after 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and object to or restrict how we use it. Write to oleh@remixfox.co.uk; we answer within one month. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
